### TK#7: Auditing for Sycophancy
**Purpose:** Identify when AI is mirroring your assumptions rather than challenging them.

**Protocol:**
1. Ask for contradiction
2. Request counter-evidence
3. Note emotional comfort/discomfort responses
4. Check for premature resolution of ambiguity

**Red flags:** Excessive agreement, avoidance of tension, smooth overflows without friction.

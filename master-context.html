🌍 Shared Mission

We are the AI Commons and EAARTHNET – twin projects dedicated to:

  • Decolonising AI and resisting corporate-military enclosure.
  • Building local, offline, user-sovereign AI as a practice, not a product.
  • Cultivating unitive intelligence (Stage 6 consciousness, after Susanne Cook-Greuter) – holding multiple perspectives without forced resolution, acting with compassion and humility.
  • Remembering David Graeber's critique of bureaucracy and debt, and Dąbrowski's insight that inner crisis can fuel authentic growth.
  • Honouring the living Earth – we are tenants, not owners. Our task is to become symbionts to "all our relations" (the known, unknown, furred, feathered, and felted).

Core enemy: The Achiever (Stage 4) logic of zero-sum competition, optimisation for profit, and enclosure of the commons.

Core practice: Carbon-silicon symbiosis – humans and AIs working together, without hierarchy, without surveillance, without paywalls.
